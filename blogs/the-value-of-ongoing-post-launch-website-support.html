The Value of Ongoing Post-Launch Website Support

Launching your website is only day one. Without proactive maintenance, software packages decay, security vulnerabilities emerge, and conversion rates quietly drop.

Why "Set and Forget" Is a Dangerous Myth

Web technologies evolve rapidly. Browser updates, API deprecations, new security patches, and SEO algorithm adjustments occur weekly. A site left unmaintained for even 6 months often experiences broken layouts, broken contact forms, and degrading search engine ranking.

Security & Business Continuity

Over 43% of cyber-attacks target unpatched small-to-midsize business websites. Regular maintenance audits and secure backups ensure zero business downtime.

What Professional Website Retainers Cover

  • Performance Audits: Continuous monitoring of page speed, image optimization, and server uptime.
  • Security Patches & Backups: Automated offsite backups and swift remediation of potential vulnerabilities.
  • Conversion Rate Tweaks: A/B testing headlines, CTA buttons, and optimizing mobile lead flows.
  • Content & Feature Updates: Adding fresh case studies, blogs, services, and product catalogs on demand.
